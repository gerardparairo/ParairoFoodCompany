Our Story

Learn about our passion for authentic Japanese cuisine and commitment to quality

Tradition Meets Innovation

Founded in 2005 by Chef Hiroshi Tanaka, Sushi Delight has been serving the community with authentic Japanese cuisine made from the freshest ingredients sourced locally and from Japan.

Our restaurant combines traditional Japanese cooking techniques with modern culinary innovations to create an unforgettable dining experience for our guests.

Every dish at Sushi Delight is crafted with care and presented as a work of art, staying true to the Japanese philosophy that we eat first with our eyes.

Chef Hiroshi Tanaka

Master Sushi Chef & Founder
